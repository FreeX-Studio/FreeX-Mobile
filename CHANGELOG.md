# Changelog

## FreeX Mobile 1.0.0

First version of the apps for FreeX Media Server on **iOS / iPadOS** (15+) and **Android** (7.0+), with one shared
interface:

- Connecting to a server by its address, with a list of recent servers; sign-in with two-factor authentication;
  switching users with a PIN
- Tab bar with home screen (the newest title large on top, then the server's rows), libraries (poster grid with
  sorting, watch-state and genre filters, the server's recommendations and collections), search, watchlist and
  settings; pull to refresh, swipe back from the left edge
- Detail pages for movies, shows and episodes: resume or start over, seasons and episodes with watch state, mark as
  watched, watchlist, versions, trailers and extras, cast with the titles of each person
- Player for touch: the phone's own player (AVPlayer on iOS, ExoPlayer on Android) plays many files directly;
  double tap to jump, seek previews and chapters, Skip intro and credits, *Next episode* with countdown, audio
  tracks, subtitles (text drawn by the app in the style from your settings, picture subtitles burned in by the
  server), quality, speed; picture in picture, lock screen and headset controls, AirPlay on iOS; progress is
  reported to the server like in FreeX Web
- Settings of the account: audio and subtitle language, subtitle mode and size, quality at home and on the go,
  display language
- German and English, following the display language of the account or the device
